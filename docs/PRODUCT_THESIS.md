## Originality gate
Before major implementation, answer:

- What already exists?
- What do users dislike about it?
- What are competitors doing the same way?
- Which conventions are good and should remain?
- Which workflow can we improve materially?
- Would the product still feel distinctive with its logo removed?
- Is each novel interaction helping the user, or merely looking unusual?
